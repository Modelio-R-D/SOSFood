Project: SOS Food (Horizon Europe, Grant Agreement No. 101134894), sosfood-project.eu

The problem

Providers upload CSV files (up to 1 GB) to food safety data lakes. These files should contain only measurements, but may hold emails or phone numbers, which become a GDPR liability once indexed. Generic PII detectors flag food data as phone numbers: GPS coordinates, EAN barcodes, lab references and nutritional decimals look like international phone formats. A blocked 1 GB file wastes the provider's effort, so false positives must be zero.

The idea: metamorphic testing

Our detector reads files line by line: email regex, phone candidate extraction, eight food-data exclusion filters (each born from a real production false positive), then phone validation with libphonenumber restricted to European regions. There is no cheap oracle for "does this line contain personal data", so we transform a known input and check that the verdict follows a rule. From 78 seeds (26 positive, 52 negative), four metamorphic relations generate 207 test inputs automatically. Adding a seed adds tests, with no manual writing.

The context must not change the verdict

MR1. A confirmed email or phone placed among food-data fields must still be blocked.

MR3. Food-data patterns combined with each other must still pass (66 pairwise combinations of 12 production rows).

Format and case must not change the verdict

MR2. A phone written with spaces, dots, hyphens, a 00 prefix, parentheses or compact is still blocked (6 variants).

MR4. An email in lower, upper, mixed case or with an upper-case domain is still blocked (4 variants).

Evaluation

500 annotated samples: 300 food data without PII, and 200 PII samples (141 in scope: emails and European international phones; 59 out of scope: names, addresses, local-format phones, etc.). 472 are synthetic, 28 come from real production uploads. Compared with Microsoft Presidio v2.2, default recognizers, same corpus.

MeasureThis systemPresidio
False positives on 300 food samples028
Precision1.000 (95% CI 0.97 to 1.0)0.833
In-scope recall0.993 (140 of 141)0.993

Precision: of everything flagged, the share that really is PII. Low precision means clean files get blocked.

Recall: of the real in-scope PII, the share that is caught. Low recall means personal data slips into the lake.

The difference on food data is significant (McNemar exact test, p < 10-8). The one in-scope miss is a French 07 number classified as VOIP by libphonenumber, which Presidio also misses. A mutation experiment (73 seeded faults) shows 78% killed by the suite. In production for 18 months on two European data lake platforms.

Caveat: 0 false positives on 300 samples bounds the false-positive rate at 1.3% (95%). Samples are variants of seed families, not a random draw from providers.
